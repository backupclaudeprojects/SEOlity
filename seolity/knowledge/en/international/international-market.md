# International SEO market playbook

## What is available outside sanctioned markets
- **Google Merchant Center** with free product listings (Shopping tab, product knowledge panels) and product feeds — a major organic channel for stores; keep feed price/availability identical to the page and the Product schema.
- **Google Business Profile** with verification: local pack, reviews, posts, products; the base of local SEO.
- **Keyword Planner** (with an Ads account), Search Console, Google Trends, and paid tools with reliable volume data per country.

## Priorities for a site competing internationally
1. **Technical health:** fast global delivery (CDN), Core Web Vitals within "good" for mobile, clean indexation (sitemap only with 200 canonical URLs), structured data valid in Rich Results Test.
2. **Search intent coverage:** one page per intent; topic clusters around commercial categories; comparison and "best" guides with first-hand testing (Google's reviews system rewards real evaluation).
3. **E-E-A-T:** author pages, expert review for YMYL topics, original photos and data, transparent business information, return policy.
4. **Links:** digital PR (original data, expert commentary, newsworthy stories), partnerships and genuine mentions. Paid placements must use rel="sponsored"; scaled link schemes are link spam.
5. **Merchant & local:** Merchant Center feed, GBP fully completed, consistent NAP across major directories for the target country.

## Multi-country and multi-language
- Use **hreflang** (return links on every version, an x-default) when the same content targets several languages or regions; use local currency, units and shipping info per version.
- Prefer subfolders (`/de/`, `/fr/`) for authority consolidation unless a ccTLD is clearly better for trust in that country.
- Never auto-redirect visitors by IP without a choice — Googlebot crawls mostly from the US and would never see other versions.

## AI search surfaces
AI Overviews and AI answer engines cite pages that answer precisely, show expertise and are easy to extract (clear headings, direct first sentences, tables, FAQs). Track visibility for key queries; keep facts current and sourced.

## Measurement
Compare equal windows before/after each change against the site-wide trend; segment by country and device in Search Console; annotate algorithm updates (Search Status Dashboard) before attributing a change.
